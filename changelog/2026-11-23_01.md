# Memory-Layers Pins Move to November 2026; Profiles Catch Up

## Why Care?

The study's profiles describe upstream code at a pinned commit. Most pins dated from May 2026, and in six months several entries changed in ways that made the profiles wrong: one changed its retrieval model, one changed its license, two stopped active development in their pinned repos, and the yardstick benchmark retracted its own numbers. Every submodule now points at the head of its upstream default branch, and every profile has been re-read against the new code.

## What's New?

- **Pins.** 17 of 18 submodules moved, by between 6 and 2,768 commits. Volt had no new commits. The README's new "Pins last refreshed 2026-11-23" section has the full table with dates, commit counts, and latest releases.
- **A "Changes since the previous pin" section in every profile**, citing paths at the new commit. Stale paths and line numbers in the body were re-located.
- **Changes worth knowing first:**
  - **Hindsight** reached 1.0 (2026-10-13) and v1.2.3. Its Claude Code plugin and the other per-agent plugins were replaced by one cross-agent package, `@vectorize-io/hindsight-coding-agents`, which keeps one memory bank per repo shared by every agent and seeds it from git history. The profile has a new section on it.
  - **OpenViking** dropped recursive directory-walk retrieval in v0.4.23; recall is now one vector search scoped to a subtree. The profile body and its index line were rewritten.
  - **Graphify** relicensed from MIT to Apache-2.0 on 2026-07-22.
  - **Letta** moved its V1 server to an `archive` branch; `main` is a landing page.
  - **Neo** was retired on 2026-11-08, with 0.63.0 as its final release.
  - **StateBench** 2.0 found six defects in its own scorer and retracted its 1.x numbers. The profile's lede, built on a 1.x result, was rewritten.
  - **Graphiti** removed its Kuzu driver and added the embedded Relate driver.
  - **ByteRover** stopped running its own LLM; the calling agent now does curate and query work.
  - **Supermemory** shipped a self-hosted engine binary, but its source is still not public.
- **Profiles index.** Ledes for ByteRover, Letta, Neo, OpenViking, StateBench, and Supermemory were updated where the old line was no longer true.

## Under the Hood

- Upstream repos were only read. Nothing was edited or committed inside them.
- Vendor benchmark figures stay attributed as vendor-reported. Star counts that could not be re-checked are marked as of the original pin.
- Only date keys and pin-describing keys changed in frontmatter, plus a `lede` or `summary` where the old text had become false.
